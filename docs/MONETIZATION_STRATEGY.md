# SleepWise Monetization Strategy

## Executive Summary

SleepWise employs a **freemium model** with four distinct tiers: Free, Premium Individual, Premium Family, and Corporate Enterprise. This strategy balances user acquisition, value delivery, and revenue optimization while maintaining ethical health data practices.

**Revenue Targets (Year 1):**
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): $500K
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): $6M
- Total Users: 500K
- Paying Customers: 25K (5% conversion)
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU): $20/month

## Pricing Tiers

### 1. FREE Tier (Freemium)

**Price:** $0

**Target:** User acquisition, trial conversion

**Features:**
- ✅ Basic sleep tracking (microphone-based)
- ✅ Sleep score calculation
- ✅ Smart alarm (wake in optimal phase)
- ✅ 7 days of sleep history
- ✅ Basic insights (limited)
- ✅ Manual sleep logging

**Limitations:**
- ❌ No historical trends beyond 7 days
- ❌ No AI predictions
- ❌ No detailed sleep phase analysis
- ❌ No health integrations (HealthKit/Google Fit)
- ❌ No family sharing
- ❌ Basic customer support only
- ❌ Ads (non-intrusive, health-related)

**Conversion Triggers:**
- After 7 days: "See your full sleep history with Premium"
- When accessing trends: "Unlock trends analysis"
- When sleep score is good: "Get AI predictions to maintain this!"
- When sleep score is poor: "AI insights can help improve your sleep"

**Business Rationale:**
- **User Acquisition**: Low barrier to entry
- **Network Effects**: More users = better ML models
- **Data Collection**: Anonymous data improves product
- **Viral Growth**: Word-of-mouth marketing

- Referral bonuses

### Value Perception
- Free tier shows "what you're missing"
- Feature comparison tables

## Conversion Optimization

### A/B Testing Strategy

**Pricing Tests:**
1. Price points: $7.99 vs $9.99 vs $12.99
2. Annual discount: 25% vs 33% vs 40%
3. Free trial length: 7 days vs 14 days vs 30 days

**Feature Tests:**
1. Free tier limitations: 7 days vs 14 days vs 30 days history
2. Paywall timing: Immediate vs after 3 sessions vs after 7 days
3. Upgrade messaging: Feature-focused vs savings-focused

**UI/UX Tests:**
1. Upgrade button placement
2. Pricing page design
3. Checkout flow steps

### Conversion Triggers

**Time-based:**
- Day 3: "You're doing great! Unlock full insights?"
- Day 7: "History expiring soon. Save with Premium?"
- Day 30: "See your monthly trends with Premium"

**Behavior-based:**
- Good sleep score: "Maintain this with AI predictions!"
- Poor sleep score: "Get personalized recommendations"
- Consistent usage: "You're serious about sleep. Go Premium?"

**Feature-based:**
- Accessing locked feature: Gentle paywall
- Viewing trends: "Unlock all periods"
- Attempting export: Premium feature

## Churn Prevention

### At-Risk Indicators
- No sleep sessions in 7 days
- Decreasing usage over 2 weeks
- Cancellation flow started
- Support tickets about "too expensive"

### Retention Strategies
- **Win-back email series**
- **Personalized discount offers**
- **Feature highlights** (unused features)
- **Success stories** from similar users
- **Pause subscription** (instead of cancel)
- **Downgrade option** (annual to monthly)

### Cancellation Flow
1. "Are you sure?" with value reminder
2. **Offer discount** (20% off for 3 months)
3. **Pause option** (3 months)
4. **Feedback survey** (why canceling?)
5. **Win-back campaign** (30/60/90 days)

## Ethical Considerations

### Data Privacy
- **No sale of personal data**
- **Anonymization for research** (opt-in only)
- **Transparent pricing** (no hidden fees)
- **Fair cancellation policy** (no auto-renewal tricks)

### Health Ethics
- **No fear-mongering** in marketing
- **Evidence-based claims only**
- **Medical disclaimer** (not diagnostic tool)
- **Doctor referrals** for serious concerns

### Accessibility
- **Free tier always available**
- **Discounts for underserved populations**
- **Accessibility features** (free for all tiers)
